Privacy policy

What data the Kinovoice website collects, why, who processes it and how to withdraw consent.

The data controller is the publisher of Kinovoice, Betsy Isakova. Questions about your data: press@kinovoice.com. Last updated 7 November 2026; this date changes whenever the policy does.

What is collected

The site is static: pages are built in advance and served as files; there are no accounts or forms asking for personal details. Dataappears in two cases: you agreed to analytics or you wrote to us.

Analytics

The site counts visits with Yandex Metrica. The only purpose is to see which texts are read, where readers come from and what on the site does not work. There is no advertising on the site and no data is collected for it.

The legal basis is your consent: pressing “Accept” in the cookie notice. Until then the Metrica script is not loaded and its cookies are not set. If you press “Decline”, the site works as usual, just without analytics.

What Metrica receives: pages viewed and the referrer, including tags in the link address; site search queries; device and browser type; approximate region by IP address; a visitor identifier in cookies (_ym_uid and others); actions on the page — clicks on Telegram links, “Share” buttons and partner publications, file downloads, reading a text to the end. Personal details are not sent.

The data is processed by Yandex LLC on our behalf on servers in Russia under the Yandex Metrica terms; we see it in aggregate form only. There is no cross-border transfer.

You can withdraw consent at any time: clear the site data in your browser (the notice reappears, press “Decline”) or install the Yandex Metrica blocker extension. To delete data already collected about your browser, write to press@kinovoice.com.

Email

Mail sent to press@kinovoice.com is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to the editorial mailbox (Google) and kept there while the correspondence lasts. Addresses are not used for mailings and are not shared with third parties.

Cookies and local storage

The site remembers your chosen theme and your answer to the cookie notice in the browser. These are technically necessary and are not sent anywhere. There are no advertising trackers.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw consent. Write to press@kinovoice.com — we reply within ten working days. Data is never sold or shared for advertising.
