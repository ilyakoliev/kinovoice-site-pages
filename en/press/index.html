About the publication

Kinovoice is an independent online publication on film, series and theatre. It publishes reviews of festival and theatrical premieres, coverage of series and streaming, theatre and book reviews and festival notes. The main language is Russian; the texts are also published in English translation.

Editor-in-chief: Betsy Isakova, a film critic and media manager who contributes to Kinopoisk, Okko, The Blueprint and RBC Style and produced Kinopoisk’s film podcast Krupnym Planom (“Close-Up”) for almost three years. The publication grew out of her Telegram channel kino_voice.
