Facts about the series

  • Season 1 is Philosopher’s Stone. Eight episodes based on the first book. The season takes the British title of the novel; in the US the first film was released as Harry Potter and the Sorcerer’s Stone.

  • The premiere moved up. When filming began in July 2025, HBO was promising a 2027 debut. On 25 March 2026, alongside the first teaser, the premiere was set for Christmas 2026. HBO chief Casey Bloys has since reaffirmed the Christmas Day launch.

  • Seven books, seven seasons. HBO plans to adapt all seven novels, one season per book, over roughly a decade. Season 2, based on Chamber of Secrets, was ordered on 6 May 2026, before season 1 had premiered, with filming set for autumn 2026. Casey Bloys says the gap between seasons will be under two years.

  • Who is making it. Showrunner Francesca Gardiner (Succession, His Dark Materials, Killing Eve) writes the series, and Mark Mylod (Succession, Game of Thrones) directed several episodes. From season 2 Gardiner co-runs the show with Jon Brown, a season 1 writer she first worked with on Succession.

  • Where and when it was filmed. At Warner Bros. Studios Leavesden in the UK. Filming began on 14 July 2025, almost 14 years to the day after Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2 closed the film series, and ran into spring 2026: in early May a couple of weeks were left.

  • The trio came from 32,000 hopefuls. That is how many young actors had auditioned for Harry, Ron and Hermione by December 2024, according to Francesca Gardiner. All three chosen leads are screen newcomers.

  • Who plays whom. Nick Frost plays Hagrid, Luke Thallon Quirrell, Paul Whitehouse Filch, Lox Pratt Draco Malfoy and Johnny Flynn his father Lucius, Katherine Parkinson Molly Weasley, and Bel Powley and Daniel Rigby the Dursleys. Warwick Davis is back as Professor Flitwick, a role he originated in the films.

  • Music by Hans Zimmer. Zimmer and his Bleeding Fingers collective are writing the score, HBO announced on 15 January 2026. Costumes are by Holly Waddington, an Oscar winner for Poor Things.
