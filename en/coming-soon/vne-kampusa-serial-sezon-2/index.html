Facts about the series

  • The date is official. Prime Video announced the season 2 premiere on 5 November 2026: 28 April 2027, in more than 240 countries and territories at once.

  • Whose season it is. The lead couple is now Allie Hayes (Mika Abdalla) and Dean Di Laurentis (Stephen Kalyn), whose romance comes from Elle Kennedy's third book, The Score. Hannah and Garrett from season 1 stay in the cast.

  • Where it was filmed. In Vancouver, from 1 June to 18 September 2026, with stages at North Shore Studios. The University of British Columbia campus stands in for the fictional Briar University, hockey games are shot at its Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre, and The Heatley bar in Strathcona plays Malone's diner.

  • Renewed early. Prime Video ordered season 2 on 12 February 2026, before season 1 came out. Creator Louisa Levy said all eight season 2 scripts were written by the time season 1 premiered.

  • Season 1 came out in May 2026. According to Amazon, 36 million people watched it in its first 12 days, and at launch it was the third most-watched debut season in Prime Video history.

  • Who's new, who's gone. India Fowler joins the season 2 regular cast. Josh Heuston, who played Justin Kohl, will not return, though Louisa Levy has not ruled out bringing the character back later.

  • Based on Elle Kennedy's books. The author has written more than 50 novels, translated into over 25 languages, with more than 10 million copies sold. Louisa Levy created the series and runs it with Gina Fattore. Temple Hill produces.
