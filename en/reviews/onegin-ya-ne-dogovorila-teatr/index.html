Onegin. I Wasn’t Finished: The Classics Have Been Taught to Really Rock

In short

“Onegin. Ya ne dogovorila” is a 2026 stage production directed by Natasha Shurganova. The classic text went through deconstruction and came together again, newly relevant and comprehensible to everyone.

BIBetsy Isakova·21 March 2026

About the production

Title
Onegin. Ya ne dogovorila · Онегин. Я не договорила
Director
Natasha Shurganova
Year
2026

Onegin. Ya ne dogovorila («Онегин. Я не договорила», “Onegin. I Wasn’t Finished”) at the Moscow theatre a39 is a total knockout.

Zoomer actors are absolutely the best thing that could have happened to theatre in the 21st century. And they, it seems, have done the best thing that could have happened to Eugene Onegin at the present moment. The classic text went through deconstruction and came together again, newly relevant and comprehensible to everyone.

The “bodily commentary”, as announced in the production’s subtitle, was there too. The utterly mind-blowing techno, to the accompaniment of which the characters and relationships of Zhenya, Tanya, Olya and Vova (yes, exactly like that: the diminutives of Eugene, Tatyana, Olga and Vladimir) unfolded, sweeps away any scepticism without trace. Because the classics have been taught to really rock. Just consider the 2 women of mature years who were googling Sydney Sweeney with interest during one of the monologues.

I watch all of a39’s premieres, and usually leave more or less satisfied. But Onegin is an absolute revelation even against the background of the other cool things at this theatre. Every time I am impressed by the boldness and innovation of these guys, and I recommend them to you with all my heart.
