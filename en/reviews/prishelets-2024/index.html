The Alien: Simple Science Fiction That Could Really Have Happened

In short

“The Alien” is a 2024 film directed by Ivan Sosnin. The film, as always with this director, instils faith in the best and charges you with kindness, and sun and bright colours break through the impassable Russian snows on screen.

About the film

Title
The Alien · Пришелец
Director
Ivan Sosnin
Year

24 October 2024

Yesterday I was at the premiere of Ivan Sosnin’s The Alien (Prishelets). The film, as always with this director, instils faith in the best and charges you with kindness, and sun and bright colours break through the impassable Russian snows on screen.

Lyosha, a young man (everyone in the village thinks he is strange, but that is not so at all), is impatiently waiting for aliens to arrive: he draws crop circles, tunes the radio equipment in his house, picks up signals and, in general, lives for his idea. In the end he does meet an alien woman, and of course that opens his eyes to life.

The science fiction here is simple, without the tiresome profusion of special effects. It is exactly how an alien might appear in an ordinary village, in the snowy Russian landscapes familiar to us. Such a deliberate avoidance of special effects makes the science fiction sweet, close, inspiring the feeling of a story that could really have happened.

The protagonist, played by Maxim Stoyanov, could not have been any different. It is a wonderful performance, a combination of vulnerability and defencelessness with a righteous anger at a world that promises people like Lyosha nothing good. Stoyanov’s physicality also works for the character: on the outside he looks like a mighty giant, but inside, as we see, he has a very fragile world, which the actor manages to convey. After The Alien I looked at this actor with new eyes; that is one of many things to thank the film for.

And also: in Ivan Sosnin’s films even villains get a chance at something better and, without meaning to, set out on the path of good. The film’s finale is proof of that.

In cinemas from today: a warm recommendation to go with your loved ones and share the bright emotions of a worthwhile film.

24 December 2024

In my colleagues’ and my year-end selection of films at Kinopoisk I, as has become the custom, answered for kindness and talked about The Alien. Ivan Sosnin’s new film is striking not so much for its thought about people’s non-acceptance of their neighbour (usual for the exposition of this author’s stories) as for the language chosen to convey this problem:

In the hands of the kindest Russian director, Ivan Sosnin, the cruel axiom “man is a wolf to man”, to a track by Mujuice, turns into a gentle but intelligible reminder: “man is an alien to man”.

If in 2022’s Dalyokie blizkie (“Distant Close Ones”) contact was made on Earth, by driving a few thousand kilometres in a van and talking heart to heart, in 2024 a whole sci-fi with an ambiguous finale is being launched. That is precisely the reason that makes any summing-up of 2024 impossible without The Alien, a snowy, chamber story that, in fact, concerns everyone.
