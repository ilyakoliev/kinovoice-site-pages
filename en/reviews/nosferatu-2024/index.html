Robert Eggers’s Nosferatu: The Most Well-Made and Successful Remake

In short

“Nosferatu” is a 2024 film directed by Robert Eggers. Robert Eggers’s Nosferatu seems to be the most well-made and successful remake I have seen.

BIBetsy Isakova·24 January 2025

About the film

Title
Nosferatu
Director
Robert Eggers
Year
2024

Robert Eggers’s Nosferatu seems to be the most well-made and successful remake I have seen. I enjoyed every minute of the film, and here is why:

  • The remake is exactly twice as long as the original, but it shows in more detail the cause-and-effect links, the characters’ motivations and other half-tones barely perceptible in the source film, and my viewing experience benefited from this. Sometimes articulating certain facts in dialogue is not chewing over the obvious but widening the context

  • Eggers’s film popularises the story of Nosferatu, which until now mostly interested a limited circle of insiders. Now the reminder that light conquers darkness is in plain view for everyone again.

  • Nicholas Hoult is the perfect Hutter. I have heard opinions that he is bland in this film, but that is precisely the perfection: his character is the real victim of this Nosferatu story, deprived of the right to decide his own fate in roughly the first scene, so being “bland” is what the logic of the character requires.

  • Lily-Rose Depp is an extremely strong actress in terms of physical performance. It is precisely the bodily aspects of her acting that gave this whole mystical story a kind of anchoring in physical reality, where the evil force is anything but a ghost.

  • Nosferatu’s new look (and peculiar speech) is an excellent find. The 1922 Nosferatu evoked only fear. In his new guise the vampire evokes a range of mixed emotions and reminds us that nothing human is alien to evil. But let us not forget that this does not stop it from being evil.
