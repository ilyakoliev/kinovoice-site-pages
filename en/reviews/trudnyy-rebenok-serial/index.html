Problem Child: Tangible Madness and an Absolute Must-Watch Series

In short

“Problem Child” is a 2026 series directed by Vladimir Bolgov, Velimir Rusakov. The series has such a perceptible, tangible air of madness and inadequacy that it plunges you into an unnatural viewer’s comfort.

About the series

Title
Problem Child · Трудный ребёнок
Director
Vladimir Bolgov, Velimir Rusakov
Year
2026

25 September 2026

I am watching Problem Child with a kind of half-forgotten zest

The series has such a perceptible, tangible air of madness and inadequacy that it plunges you into an unnatural viewer’s comfort. Perhaps it would even be appropriate to call it “ecstasy”: wow, someone on screen has it even more unhinged than in real life. But even in such an upside-down, strange world there is room for love (all ages and social strata really are subject to it), friendship and optimism. And now the story of an injured footballer who moonlights as a tutor to pay for an operation and return to the sport plays in new colours.

And all the actors are exactly in their places: I will now definitely be following the projects of Gennady Blinov (the footballer-historian) and Boris Deikov (the problem child himself).

A wonderful piece of work, and one with individuality against the background of many comedy series.

8 October 2026

I have finished Problem Child, and now I can say with confidence that it is an absolute must-watch series.

Besides what has already been praised in the posts above, something else needs noting. This is a rare case of a series whose pace does not sag or break in the middle of the season (a rarity these days): there is no urge to drop it or skip ahead to the end. Form here is in complete harmony with content, absurdity in both, but very well balanced. A separate bow to the screenwriters for the genuine urge to keep a notebook of quotes while watching.

And the footballer-historian joins my personal collection of characters in the category “you don’t want to be with him, you want to be him”. Stay tuned.
