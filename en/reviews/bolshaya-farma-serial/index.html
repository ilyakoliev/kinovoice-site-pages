Big Pharma: The Pleasure of Visual Language

About the series

Title
Big Pharma · Большая фарма
Director
Danil Chashchin
Year
2026

I have started Big Pharma, and I am getting real, sincere pleasure from the visual language of this series.

Cinematographer Batyr Morgachev uses a fisheye lens here, and wide-angle perspective, and swirling bokeh (that blurred background around Goroshko that seems to curl in motion). You cannot take your eyes off it, in short.

All this, plus the actors’ well-aimed performances, compensates me for a slight sense of contrivance over how richly the main characters live: by every indication a no-name researcher who spent a couple of decades working on a single drug, and his plastic-surgeon son, whose prosperity one believes in, of course, but not to that extent. Nevertheless, I will definitely keep watching: this is an extremely gripping pharmaceutical thriller.
