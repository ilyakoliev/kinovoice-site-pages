Den radio: A Live Look at What We Are Used to Seeing as a Film

In short

“Den radio” is a 2001 stage production directed by Sergey Petreykov. The production is good precisely as a live look at what we are used to seeing in the fixed format of film.

BIBetsy Isakova·26 May 2025

About the production

Title
Den radio · День радио
Director
Sergey Petreykov
Year
2001

Another theatrical off-topic post. This time about a production that I had rewatched as a film some 8 times.

It is Den radio («День радио», “Radio Day”), a show that Kvartet I (the Moscow comedy troupe) have been performing for 25 years now and that has existed as a film since 2008. The production is good precisely as a live look at what we are used to seeing in the fixed format of film. On stage the intonations are livelier and the characters more three-dimensional; I was especially impressed by Nonna Grishaeva, who seems not to have changed at all over these 25 years) By the way, every signature “maladtsy!” (“well done!”; put a ❤️ if you read it in that very intonation)) from Leonid Barats’s character is met with applause by the audience, and it is great to see that with your own eyes. But make sure you do not know all the jokes from the film literally by heart before you go, otherwise the wow effect is somewhat dulled

Why go to the theatre, you will ask, when there is both the film and a television version of the play? One nuance: in neither of these versions will you see the magnificent Pavel Maykov performing songs as the frontmen of several bands. Every musical number is an explosion of energy, comedy and artistry from Maykov and the band Bobry. It was this artist’s participation in the production that was the deciding factor for me: Maykov does not have many theatre roles, and watching him perform is always a great pleasure.
