Poker for Us: A Story That Ended Purely by Karma

In short

“Poker for Us” is a 2025 series directed by Rustam Ilyasov. I am as pleased with the series as can be and will be waiting intently for news of a second season (by every parameter and every hook cast, it is essential here).

BIBetsy Isakova·20 June 2025·updated 3 December 2025

About the series

Title
Poker for Us · Нам покер
Director
Rustam Ilyasov
Year
2025

20 June 2025

Fun and illegal, like gambling, the project Poker for Us (Nam poker) wins you over with stylish camerawork and crisp portraits of its characters from the first frame. Respect, by the way, for the pop-up explanations of gaming terms!

Three friends decide to open a poker club right in their own home, but the illegal business draws them in. The spirit of illicit fun is infectious through the screen, and now we are already rooting for the hapless owners of the gambling den.

The cast includes Artyom Koshman, Sasha Bortich and Pyotr Skvortsov (always glad to see him). As in Popados (“Busted”), the consequences of stepping onto the slippery slope for young people are declared from the first frame. But here my favourite mechanism works: even knowing the outcome, it is extremely curious to find out how the heroes came to live like this.

6 November 2025

The very likeable series Poker for Us also launched with two episodes.

I fell for it back in the summer at Pilot (the Russian series festival), and the review of the first episode was written right from there. The second does not slacken the pace either. In particular, Pyotr Skvortsov’s character, mysteriously announced in the first episode, unfolds further into full-blown madness. I have not seen the actor in this role of a completely unhinged villain for a long time, and it suits him very well, so the creators of Poker for Us have hit the jackpot here.

The very texture of the story about two students caught in an unhealthy scheme with an illegal casino is very absorbing, because it is done briskly, in a contemporary way and not as dramatically as it could have been with such a plot. Besides, the effect of the half-open ending announced in the first frames of the series immediately makes you want to find out how they came to live like this.

3 December 2025

Poker for Us is over.

I am as pleased with the series as can be and will be waiting intently for news of a second season (by every parameter and every hook cast, it is essential here).

Yesterday, finishing the final episode, I became definitively convinced of the thought that nothing more plausible in terms of fatalities developing in parallel in the lives of specific people has been filmed in recent years (or I have not seen it). I do not run an underground casino, of course, but all the heroes’ ordeals from the endless cycle of problems flowing out of one another I can quite understand and share. Luckily, the story in the series ended purely by karma, so there is always hope.

Egor as played by Timofey Eletsky is the best to my taste, a very deep and, for all his deceptive exterior, touchingly sweet character. Simply a one hundred percent hit on a good man who means well and because of that sometimes does badly, and you believe it. Ruslan Bratov’s (the director of the wonderful Express) Javid is simply a riot too: evidently the axiom “a talented person is talented in everything” worked here. Vitya (Artyom Koshman) has a wonderful character arc with the strongest transformations, also a sheer pleasure to watch, as, incidentally, does Alexandra Bortich. In humour the screenwriters simply hit the jackpot: it is worth rewatching with a notebook and copying the jokes out into your personal collection.

In short, I can praise the series for a long time, as you can see; I hope as many people as possible appreciate it. I would not want it to end after one season without ever revealing to us the context of the video message from Koshman’s character in the first episode.
