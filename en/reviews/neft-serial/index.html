Oil: A Comedy About Bootleg Oil Extraction with Perfect Timing

In short

“Oil” is a series directed by Alexey Shabarov. The premiere of the series Oil, a comedy about bootleg oil extraction from Premier and Start, has interesting timing from a news point of view.

BIBetsy Isakova·31 August 2026

About the series

Title
Oil · Нефть
Director
Alexey Shabarov

The premiere of the series Oil (Neft), a comedy about bootleg oil extraction from Premier and Start (Russian streaming services), has interesting timing from a news point of view.

The teaser retells the plot briefly and to the point: Semyon (Anton Vasilyev), a working man, decides to change his life in order to win back his wife (Elena Nikolaeva). He is helped by an oil well unexpectedly discovered in his own water well and by his eccentric neighbour Ulyan (Ivan Dobronravov).

My main bet for these 8 episodes is the borderline state as performed by Anton Vasilyev. He did it very well in the recent Bolshaya farma (“Big Pharma”), albeit in a supporting role, so I have no doubts here either. We will hear the premiere date soon.
