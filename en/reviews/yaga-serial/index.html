Yaga: A Supernatural Mystery for a Gloomy Autumn

About the series

Title

Year

The first episodes of the series Yaga are finally out

This is a very solid, very compact supernatural mystery. For a gloomy autumn it is downright perfect. Private detective Rapp arrives in a remote small town in search of Henry Park, the missing son of a wealthy family, and plunges headlong into the local intrigues. Only nobody is especially eager to help him: not the local police officer Carson, not Professor Katherine, nor the missing Henry’s roommate (who for some reason is his spitting image; both characters are played by Hudson Williams). And then all sorts of supernatural phenomena make themselves known: not a minute’s peace for the characters or for the viewers.

The text of the play, which I had got through in advance, is used to the full. But even better is the part of the action built on top of it, developed by the series’ writers. The two episodes fly by in a single breath, and I hope the rest are made in the same spirit. The IC3PEAK song (the band has been designated a “foreign agent” in Russia) in the neon party scene fits perfectly. Evidently, Russian songs at raves are now a must-have for series on the streaming service Crave.
