Rag Union: A Half-Forgotten but Heart-Warming Film

In short

“Rag Union” is a 2015 film directed by Mikhail Mestetskiy. In short, the atmosphere of a summer adventure whose reality you doubt for the rest of your life is guaranteed.

BIBetsy Isakova·31 January 2026

About the film

Title
Rag Union · Тряпичный союз
Director
Mikhail Mestetskiy
Year
2015

I am picking up the #KinoFlashback relay from Olya Belik’s KinoKotik (thank you) to tell you about a slightly forgotten but heart-warming film.

I thought for a long time and in the end decided to choose Mikhail Mestetskiy’s Rag Union (Tryapichny soyuz). A film from 2014, not that old, but by my feeling not nearly watched enough by everyone)

Here we have a young Ivan Yankovsky, Alexander Pal and Pavel Chinaryov, quite unlike how we are used to seeing them. They play three charismatic rebels who make up a cell of actionist artists. By chance they meet Vanya (Vasily Butkevich), an ordinary lad who, thrilled with his new pals, is eager to take part in all their schemes (anything is better than advertising gravestones).

Judging by the events of the film, it turns out that the life of actionists is pretty eventful, and not always with art. So there is room here for a comedy of the absurd, wonderful black humour, a rom-com (with the appearance of female company) and the pithy motto “Push one thing and another falls”, which you want to take into your life after watching (which, in fact, I did).

In short, the atmosphere of a summer adventure whose reality you doubt for the rest of your life is guaranteed.

I pass the nostalgic relay on to the channels Sedmoe Iskusstvo and Bloknotik o kino: I am sure your film flashbacks will be impressive.
