Yura Was Here: A Film About Beautiful People, Beauty of the Soul and Love for One’s Neighbour

In short

“Yura Was Here” is a 2025 film directed by Sergey Malkin. Do not think this is some sentimental sweetness: on the contrary, the film is punk, dynamic, out of the ordinary.

BIBetsy Isakova·8 October 2025

About the film

Title
Yura Was Here · Здесь был Юра
Director
Sergey Malkin
Year
2025

Yura Was Here (Zdes byl Yura) is an unimaginably beautiful film about beautiful people, beauty of the soul and love for one’s neighbour, which saves first of all the one who loves. Do not think this is some sentimental sweetness: on the contrary, the film is punk, dynamic, out of the ordinary.

Onto the heads of the heroes, young musicians, lands Yura (Konstantin Khabensky), the uncle of one of the guys, who because of a birth injury can neither speak nor live independently at all. To the heroes’ difficulties with their neighbour in a communal flat and their attempts to break onto the big stage is added the care of a relative who at first seems to be needed by nobody. In time it is he who becomes the most important person for all the participants in the story.

The artistic world of the film is filled with music, colours and details that bring everything to life. I recognised in the frame, for instance, the cool café Chifanka in Moscow’s Kitay-Gorod)

I cannot help confessing my love for the songs of the band Kick Chill as performed by Kuzma Kotrelev; the creators promise to release a separate soundtrack. This, in my opinion, is the hit of the film festival, as the song “Allo, Marina” from the film God rozhdeniya (“Year of Birth”) once was. Things are fine with references here too: at the moment the candles on the cake are lit, for example, someone says the phrase “Everyone out of the Gloom”, for which Khabensky’s character from Night Watch is famous.

I will go and watch it a second time as soon as it is in cinemas: from 5 February, courtesy of @bosforpictures and @volgafilms.
