Fisher. Eclipse: Greatness in Its Purest Form

In short

“Fisher. Zatmenie” is a 2025 series directed by Alexander Tsoi. The resolution looks quick, rhythmic and without unnecessary preludes, but with an elegant sprinkling of devilry.

BIBetsy Isakova·29 May 2025·updated 18 July 2025

About the series

Title
Fisher. Zatmenie · Фишер. Затмение
Director
Alexander Tsoi
Year
2025

29 May 2025

Fisher. Zatmenie («Фишер. Затмение», “Fisher. Eclipse”, the second season of the hit crime series) pleases from the first episode: Bokov is in excellent form, Yatsenko in his role wisecracks like a god, and Starshenbaum’s heroine looks right at home in this company.

The first frames, where Bokov interrogates Fisher in prison, are as if straight out of the world of Autsors, (“Outsource”) and evoke a pleasant feeling of recognition (if that is intentional, cool). The exposition works like clockwork: there is a tail end of the old case, an extended (despite the episode’s running time of only 40 minutes!) introduction of new characters, and a humanising of the characteristic traits of the old ones.

Bokov, who kept saying “Sho?” (“What?”) in season 1, for instance, has acquired a little dog, naturally named Sho. I hope the doggie does not come to harm in the course of catching the maniac.

20 June 2025

Even in festival conditions I could not miss the new episode of Fisher: the midpoint of the season whets the appetite for the resolution to the maximum.

Besides the magnificently worked-out plot (to my taste, more powerful than in the first season!), the series’ speech specifics are gaining momentum too. This is the most linguistically distinctive series of recent times. Outdone, perhaps, only by El Ruso, where the actors talked like Old Believers)

But the virtuoso use of profanity by Yankovsky’s and Starshenbaum’s characters makes you want to literally write it down and quote it. Whichever way you look at it, such speech adds a hefty dose of sincerity and realism to everything that happens, which is extremely important for this crime story.

26 June 2025

Fisher is not only breaking records on the Kinopoisk Pro Index but also sharply (and, like Bokov, boldly) turning off the planned plot trajectory.

Having “slipped”, as Bokov put it (I never stop admiring him) at the very end of the fresh episode, the investigation is forced to change direction sharply? One would like to hope that the outcome of the investigation will not boil down to the formula “the butler did it” and that the criminal will not be as close at hand as he now seems.

And this episode has more of Yatsenko’s character and of Fisher himself. Andrey Maksimov is an amazing actor, of course: before watching the pilot episode of Normalny (“Normal”), where he plays a man on the autism spectrum, I could not imagine him in the role of even a remotely positive character (not a gangster and not a maniac). And now, what do you think? I cannot perceive him in Fisher as a maniac: he is such a sweet, kind guy. But all this, of course, is a joke; this is how I praise stunning transformations and a 180-degree change of type.

5 July 2025

Meanwhile the second season of Fisher has decided after all to be more cruel than the first and to take my favourite character away from me.

In the sixth episode everything twists mightily around one particular character whom I had come to love over the season, and it seems it will not end well. On the other hand, over the next two episodes I will definitely have time to enjoy how well the screenwriters have brought together a heap of storylines that at first seemed disparate. Sectarians, a nasty official, local gangsters: all this turns out to be intertwined and involved in the approaching resolution.

Also, incidentally, Andrey Maksimov seems to have been lucky this season to play every facet of Fisher, and it looks very, very convincing. And most importantly, the line with his interrogations does not seem at all extraneous or foreign to what is going on in Kurortny; on the contrary, it seems to let us look retrospectively at the search for the new maniac and build our own guesses based on their (the maniacs’) logic.

17 July 2025

In my opinion, the finale of the series Fisher. Zatmenie is greatness in its purest form. Besides all manner of praise for the work itself, I will follow with great interest the future roles of Nikita Khudyakov, who played Vanya Zlobin, because in this story he showed himself to be a very powerful artist. Watching him was a real pleasure.

The resolution looks quick, rhythmic and without unnecessary preludes, but with an elegant sprinkling of devilry ➡️ For me this is the perfect outcome: I do not like detective stories where the intrigue drags on and on, forcing us to watch the investigators’ train of thought for an indecently long time; personally I find it easy to drop such viewing. To the last I wished that this would not happen with Fisher, and my wishes came true. I had guesses, but in reality everything is done more cunningly, literally as unpredictable as fate itself, and that, it seems, is the height of craftsmanship. I liked the detail of Zlobin’s transfer “from somewhere up north”: Fisher has more in common with Autsors than it seems.

Separately, I cannot help admiring the character posters: bamboo, a mourning ribbon, bees; a good set of details both for those who have not yet watched and for those who already know everything.

Naturally, my book of quotations did not go without additions from Bokov’s lexicon. The line about how “I don’t need to say it twice” I will remember for a long time. May this feature continue in the future, if Fisher becomes an anthology with an immortal Bokov from season to season.

18 July 2025

The more I think about the resolution of the second Fisher, the more additional meanings I find.

First, I read an interview with the screenwriters, Sergei Kalvarsky and Natalya Kapustina. It turns out we owe the atmosphere of the series to Assa. Those who know me are aware: It is my most beloved film, but not once while watching did I catch myself making the association, and now it has hiiit me. That is what explains this convincing counterpoint that makes you suspect rot even in the sweetest character.

On the other hand, I have plenty of cultural associations regarding Zlobin, who split into twins, and that is my second point. In the few seconds they were shown on screen together, I managed to recall both Cronenberg and Balabanov. The former because of the film Dead Ringers, about twins who villainously swapped places at work and in life (literally, even with women). And from Balabanov, the screenplay My Brother Died, with its telling title and a truly Balabanovian plot. And most importantly, a resolution that quite mirrors that of Fisher.
