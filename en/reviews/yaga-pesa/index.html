Kat Sandler’s Play Yaga: Baba Yaga Without the Stereotypes

In short

“Yaga” is a 2019 book by Kat Sandler. In Kat Sandler’s play the Baba Yaga we know is not an exotic souvenir on the shelf of Canadian drama but a full-blooded, contemporary heroine.

BIBetsy Isakova·19 October 2026

About the book

Title
Yaga
Author
Kat Sandler
Year
2019

While waiting for the series Yaga with Hudson Williams. I read the play it is based on and came away thoroughly fulfilled

I adore it when Russian culture is woven into Western works without the crude stereotypes (what we call “cranberry”), and even more so when such things get a screen adaptation.

In Kat Sandler’s play the Baba Yaga we know is not an exotic souvenir on the shelf of Canadian drama but a full-blooded, contemporary heroine who thinks about her own attractiveness, craves the company of handsome men and gives off the kind of woman power you rarely see anywhere. The text has absorbed the morphology of the fairy tale and the standards of contemporary playwriting as if these two traditions had been made for each other.

For anyone planning to watch the supernatural true-crime series from 23 October, it is almost required reading. And for everyone who is simply interested in contemporary literature, I just recommend it highly: in many ways a unique experience)
