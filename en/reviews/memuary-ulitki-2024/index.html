Memoir of a Snail: An Epistolary Novel on Screen

In short

“Memoir of a Snail” is a 2024 film directed by Adam Elliot. This animated film, unlike Flow (the winner in its category), cannot be called soothing.

BIBetsy Isakova·18 March 2025

About the film

Title
Memoir of a Snail
Director
Adam Elliot
Year
2024

I am just catching up on last year’s nominees: yesterday my choice fell on Memoir of a Snail

This animated film, unlike Flow (the winner in its category), cannot be called soothing. Before us is a rather traumatic story about separated twins, a brother and a sister who lost their family early in life and then dreamed for half their lives of being reunited at least with each other, sharing their misadventures in letters. Of the two, the main heroine is the sister, Grace, but what happens can quite fairly be called an epistolary novel on screen, which is not such a frequent thing in general. The handcrafted style of stop-motion animation is also rather rare: though Adam Elliot, the author of Memoir, has already won an Oscar for the short Harvie Krumpet in this technique, to my taste the style increases the viewer’s discomfort at what they see. On the other hand, the level of empathy for the characters increases many times over too.

Nevertheless, the film draws you in, because everyone will find something of their own in this diary-like family story. Some will recognise themselves in Grace’s escape from reality (she painstakingly collects everything to do with snails), some, on the contrary, will share the romantic spirit of Gilbert, burning with the dream of becoming a fire-eater, and some will take to the worldly wisdom of the third important heroine, old Pinky, who knows how to treasure the moment.

Personally, at the end of the viewing I wrote down precisely her words:

Snails never go back the way they came, they always crawl onwards
