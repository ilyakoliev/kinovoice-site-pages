Comeback: The Air of an Era That Breathes Genuine Youth Through the Screen

In short

“Comeback” is a 2025 series directed by Dinar Garipov. The action takes place in the 2000s, and the air of the era literally breathes through the screen with some kind of genuine youth.

BIBetsy Isakova·11 October 2025·updated 16 November 2025

About the series

Title
Comeback · Камбэк
Director
Dinar Garipov
Year
2025

11 October 2025

I have got round to Comeback (Kambek), an ultra-atmospheric series with Alexander Petrov as a homeless man who has lost his memory.

The action takes place in the 2000s, and the air of the era literally breathes through the screen with some kind of genuine youth. This atmosphere is intensified by the teenage protagonists: strong, brave, wise in their own way. And it is all scored with tracks by Mujuice, who always reaches right to the depths of the soul. And the plot, of course, from episode to episode emphasises something unhackneyed about friendship, kindness to one’s neighbour and to oneself. Simple and tasteful.

Watching it, I felt things long forgotten since Mir! Druzhba! Zhvachka!, (“Peace! Friendship! Bubblegum!”, the 1990s-set series) so I am with this series to the end.

18 October 2025

I continue to watch adore Comeback

The fourth episode of the series is a perfect example of a situation where the vertical plot of an episode complements the overall horizontal structure of the story so organically that it is even hard to separate them. Gratitude to the creators and to @russian_c0de for such craftsmanship, of course.

The series is at its midpoint and is very competently gaining momentum. The record viewing figures say so eloquently. Such demand, it seems to me, can be explained by interest in the chosen era. We all, to one degree or another (especially younger viewers), came out of the 2000s, out of that culture, that reference point in our worldview and sense of self. And recalling one’s relative youth is always absorbing.

20 October 2025

I am separately enjoying the aesthetics of the school the heroes of Comeback attend

For me these are home landscapes. The main value is that it is an ordinary school (even if that came about only because of the period: in 2004 all schools were like that). I studied at exactly this type of school, and my eye rejoices at the familiar layout of the classrooms with those peeling wooden chairs (which forever snagged your tights, the girls will understand me), the worn linoleum, the wooden shelving, the wide windowsills and the chaotic plants on them. The canteen is a little bigger than in the still from the series, but with the same serving line of metal vessels with red lettering. In my last years of school, the chalkboards were replaced by whiteboards and then partly by electronic ones, but everything around remained as it was)

These days you hardly ever see such schools on screen, certainly not in stories about our time: everywhere there is bound to be a swimming pool, a canteen with a buffet like a hotel, and ultra-fashionable renovations. Such schools really do exist, but schools of the old type exist and operate in parallel (I went to mine last year, and the authenticity is intact). And it is very pleasant to recognise on screen a piece of one’s personal youth, which, it turns out, can even serve as a film set.

31 October 2025

In the sixth episode of Comeback many storylines are very neatly written off, precisely to place the emphasis on the new input in the form of Alexey Chadov’s mysterious character. Obviously it is he who will be one of the main protagonists in the two final episodes, and for that a little extra artistic and running-time space is needed.

Separately pleasing is the quotation of a scene from the film Brother. If you remember, at a certain point Danila there tried to stand up for Sveta, the tram driver, against her husband, who was beating her. Having neutralised the man, Danila asked her to leave with him, but she stayed with her spouse, who was suffering from a bullet in his leg and would obviously go on beating her. The same thing, almost frame for frame, was repeated in Comeback, and, one might say, has already reached the level of an archetypal plot.

By the way, I am watching the series very attentively in order to record every episode in a Kinopoisk feature; if you find yourself pondering the details, drop in.

16 November 2025

And the best adornment of the finale of Comeback is the news of a second season of the series.

In the last episode the concentration of events reached an off-the-scale level, but, pleasingly, not one of the storylines suffered for it. Though I preferred the first half of the story, where, first, the kids seemed to still have everything ahead of them (😭), and second, there was enough time to reflect on each event. But the last episodes have their own drive too, combined with the feeling of a departing era, of a great adventure and the carefree early 2000s.

What will I expect from season 2? Updated images of the kids (in particular, the development of Dasha’s affairs of the heart with various candidates and with Petya, who vanished as suddenly as he appeared). A non-glossy look of the late 00’s, in the series’ tradition, naturally. And the normalisation of Kompot’s life: showing the hardships of a homeless man’s life is half the job; far harder is to reflect honestly the difficulties of his socialisation and of overcoming the stigma trailing from his former status.

It is cool that the project has deservedly got a continuation, and one really does want to wait for it.
