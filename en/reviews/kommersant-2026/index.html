In the film The Hustler (Kommersant) everything is stunning, but the ending slightly disappoints (me)

There is a lot that is cool in the story of a banking bigwig who, through a combination of not the most pleasant circumstances, ends up in prison.

1️⃣ First, every 15 minutes (literally) a new calamity comes crashing down on Alexander Petrov’s character: arrest, prison, betrayal and onwards to worse. This looks not only gripping but realistic: there are situations in life that you cannot shake off for years, because they only grow with every attempt to resolve them.

2️⃣ Second, the acting, artistic and visual parts work simply perfectly for the script: convincing enough, and at the same time cinematic enough not to turn it all into a mockumentary.

3️⃣ Third, as a separate point I want to draw attention to the character played by Husky (the rapper), Slava the smotryashchiy (the inmate who runs the cell block). The last time I saw such a distinctive hit on the gangster type, right down to the intonation, was Ruzil Minekaev in The Boy’s Word.

As for the finale. I will not spoil it, but the idea that the hero supposedly chose the right path in life and did not fall below human dignity strikes me as inconsistent. In the second half of the film he literally kicks a prison-cell enemy senseless, because the situation and the basic notion of self-defence demanded it. Was that not a spiritual fall and the most genuine revenge? So the protagonist does know how to show “reciprocity” towards his enemies? Yet towards the business partner who cheated him out of money and several lost years of his life, mercy, apparently, got activated. In short, the resolution is beautiful, but it definitely does not match the realism of the rest of the picture.

P.S.: A thought keeps nagging at me. At the end the protagonist says that he never saw his former friend again, and at the decisive moment of their showdown on the outside Slava the smotryashchiy seemed to meet him halfway in an absurd impulse not to kill. It is quite likely that off screen the fate of the “friend” was decided after all, just not before Andrey’s eyes.
