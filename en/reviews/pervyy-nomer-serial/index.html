First Issue: The New Glossy Magazines Are Telegram Channels

In short

“First Issue” is a 2024 series directed by Klim Kozinsky. But what it actually turned out to be about is that the new glossy magazines are Telegram channels, influencers and their blogs and blog-lets of various calibres

5 December 2024

A checklist for watching the series First Issue: (Pervy nomer)

  • Make sure it has been long enough since you watched the series The Thaw, because here Evgeny Tsyganov got exactly the same role (only instead of the cameraman Khrustalyov it is the writer Inozemtsev), and you may get bored

  • Make sure it has been long enough since you watched the series Besprintsipnye (“The Unprincipled”), because the chronotope of Moscow for the rich is the same here, and you may get bored

  • Make sure you have no experience of working in contemporary Russian media, otherwise you may not believe the details of newsroom life on screen, where half the editorial staff walk out in a single day on principle (and presumably pay their living costs with those principles too)

  • Make sure you are not watching the series Give a Show! in parallel, because there will be an inevitable urge to compare two stories about the backstage of the media

  • But at the same time! Leave yourself time to watch the credits (they are gorgeous here) and pay attention to the supporting characters: there are wonderful characters from Denis Vlasenko, Lukerya Ilyashenko, Pavel Derevyanko and Egor Koreshkov

6 January 2025

I have finished watching the series First Issue and am amazed at the irony that befell it. The plot is supposedly devoted to glossy magazines: there is a magazine that has to be resurrected relaunched, editors and publishers, media, God forgive me, holdings, and potent newsroom ratting…

But what it actually turned out to be about is that the new glossy magazines are Telegram channels, influencers and their blogs and blog-lets of various calibres. Everything alive, active and intrigue-creating in the series happened on the internet: the magazine’s fate was made up of what was leaked, which interviews were taken, which posts were written and which stories were posted. And the magazine seemed to be just a decoration on the table at which everyone sat down to discuss completely different things.

If this effect was intended on purpose, that is cool!

P.S.: Of all the characters, my love in this series is Lukerya Ilyashenko’s heroine. What a dark, hypnotic, wicked charisma she has, how good she is in the roles of traitresses, schemers and witches. She first charmed me in the series Happy End and once again in First Issue. I waited for her appearances on screen more than for anything else.
