Death of a Unicorn: An Epic Comedy of the Absurd with a Critique of the Present

In short

“Death of a Unicorn” is a 2025 film directed by Alex Scharfman. Besides the horror component familiar from other hits of the A24 studio, Death of a Unicorn stands out for the irony in whose atmosphere everything happens.

BIBetsy Isakova·6 May 2025

About the film

Title
Death of a Unicorn
Director
Alex Scharfman
Year
2025

Yesterday Death of a Unicorn appeared on streaming services: an epic comedy of the absurd with a critique of the present and horror moments.

Despite a stock, sketch-like set-up (a careerist father at odds with his enlightened daughter, though both have been traumatised by a shared loss), the film grows stronger both in script and in ideas with the appearance of the unicorn itself. Besides the horror component familiar from other hits of the A24 studio, Death of a Unicorn stands out for the irony in whose atmosphere everything happens. Yes, there are the usual concerns of late capitalism, social inequality and ecological catastrophe, but with a smirk that takes the humour to blackness and complete absurdity. To my taste, such moves let you avoid taking the film too seriously, and therefore think over the problems shown in a calmer format.

Incidentally, the new release is in many ways reminiscent of Mickey 17 in its madcap image of the powers that be and other details. In Death of a Unicorn at some point it even comes to choosing a kind of “expendables” (exactly the role Pattinson played in Mickey 17). So it turns out to be an unexpected recommendation not only for fans of contemporary horror and social themes but also for lovers of Korean cinema with black humour and a dystopian mood.
