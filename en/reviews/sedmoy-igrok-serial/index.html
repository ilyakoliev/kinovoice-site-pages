The Seventh Player: Ted Lasso, Hockey-Style

In short

About the series

Title
The Seventh Player · Седьмой игрок
Director
Sergey Pikalov
Year
2026

I have started watching The Seventh Player: art imitates life, Ted Lasso the KHL (Russia’s Kontinental Hockey League), as they say.

Judging by the first episode, the main feeling so far is one of bustle, but one thing is clear: this time Anton Lapenko has mostly been given “himself in the given circumstances” to play rather than one of the stock types (those, admittedly, are handled by the other characters). This is rather interesting, because his character keeps standing out in the plot too, through his “outsider” status, whether in relation to his wealthy family or to his own hockey team. So there is at least one reason to follow the action.

But I very much want to believe that there will be slightly fewer music-video-style scenes further on, because they dilute the pace a great deal, and there are other ways to create atmosphere. For example, the rather well-shot inserts of hockey matches.
