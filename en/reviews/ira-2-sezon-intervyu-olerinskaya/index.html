Ingrid Olerinskaya on the Second Season of Ira

In short

“Ira” is a work Alim Beschokov, Sergey Notarius. Taking up an invitation to the set from Norm Production, I asked Ingrid Olerinskaya about the heroine and the surprises of the coming season

BIBetsy Isakova·12 September 2025

About

Title
Ira · Ира
Director
Alim Beschokov, Sergey Notarius
Year
2023

Filming of the second season of Ira is a wrap!

Taking up an invitation to the set from Norm Production, I asked Ingrid Olerinskaya about the heroine and the surprises of the coming season.

What is impossible to imagine your day on set without?

Without coffee, without my phone, because a heap of work questions keeps pouring in and the chat never goes quiet, and without my facial expression. Which quite often coincides with Ira’s.

What have you taught Ira, and vice versa?

For me this is the first role where my heroine gets pregnant, then gives birth to a child, and has a great deal of interaction with children. All of that I taught Ira, because I have 3 nephews. I have brought them up since they were small, you could say. Ira, on the other hand, taught me endurance. I am a tough little person as it is, let us say, of iron temper. But in this case, of course, the volume of work on set makes you pull yourself together and hold on to the last.

Do you think Ira will make a good mother?

Ira has every chance of becoming a good mother. Despite her relationship with the world, which we remember from season 1, in season 2 there is nevertheless a transformation and an acceptance of the situation and of her current status. And, of course, love and maternal feeling are there one way or another. I think it will develop and prevail.

Which moments on set are the hardest and, conversely, your favourites?

The most uncomfortable and infuriating moment in acting is the enormous days when I am the only one talking in the frame. On the other hand, it is also a buzz, because not everyone can do that. I pray this skill never goes anywhere. And the biggest buzz, of course, is all of Ira’s fantasies. Most of them, let us say. There is always a little magic moment there, with costume changes, with costumes, with make-up. I think it is really cool, especially when you then watch it in the context of the whole series, it is very funny. I especially like it where I portray some men. There was a sultan, a sheikh in season 1. Thomas Edison. And in season 2 there will be, I will tell you a secret, a fantasy cornet, my favourite funny character.

What would you like to pass on to the viewers of the second season of Ira?

When watching season 2, just pay attention to the script, the dialogue, the humour and in general the interesting construction and shooting. Non-linear, as if made up of flashbacks and fantasy. I think it is an interesting move; I believe in our team and I believe that the second season will be even cooler than the first.
