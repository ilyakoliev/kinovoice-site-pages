Red Rocket: The Story of a Textbook “Superfluous Man”

In short

“Red Rocket” is a 2021 film directed by Sean Baker. By and large, what we have is the story of a textbook “superfluous man” trying with all his might to make himself.

BIBetsy Isakova·20 May 2025

About the film

Title
Red Rocket
Director
Sean Baker
Year
2021

On Saturday I went to a cycling festival and rode the whole Garden Ring (Moscow’s inner ring road), and that same evening I watched a film whose protagonist also gets around mainly by bike. Coincidence? I don’t think so

I had kept Red Rocket in mind for a long time: I liked Anora so much that I wanted to get to know something else from Sean Baker’s filmography. My high expectations were fully justified: the picture turned out to be an impressive, many-layered story of an antihero towards whom you feel completely opposite emotions for all 2 hours. Thanks to the clear division of the stages of the moral fall of Mikey, the protagonist, the film becomes a truly unique emotional attraction that the viewer, willy-nilly, lives through.

By and large, what we have is the story of a textbook “superfluous man” (the classic figure of Russian literature) trying with all his might to make himself. And the main thing Sean Baker emphasises among the reasons for his failure is the closedness of a society bound by norms and unwilling to socialise a former porn actor. It is precisely the silent refusal of any socially approved work that pushes Mikey onto the slippery path we watch for the next 2 hours. And on that slippery path (yes, yes, the one Mikey travels on his bicycle) acquiring the prefix “anti-” to the title “hero” could not be easier. The question of whether the winner takes it all, as the song “The Winner Takes It All” goes, hangs as an ominous pause in the finale. (And, it seems, the answer to it can partly be guessed already in the director’s next film, Anora, which continues the same theme of social outcasts).

By the way, do not watch on an empty stomach, buy some doughnuts: you are guaranteed to want them)
