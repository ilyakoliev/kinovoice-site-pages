Raz malchishka, dva malchishka: Short in Length, but Very Palpable Reading

In short

“Raz malchishka, dva malchishka” is a 2025 book by Asya Demishkevich. I do not know how to characterise its genre more precisely: it is a coming-of-age novel, folk horror and topical allegorical absurdity all at once.

BIBetsy Isakova·4 July 2026

About the book

Title
Raz malchishka, dva malchishka · Раз мальчишка, два мальчишка
Author
Asya Demishkevich
Year
2025

I do not very often like anything from contemporary literary fiction in Russian, but recently, on a recommendation, I read Asya Demishkevich’s novel Raz malchishka, dva malchishka («Раз мальчишка, два мальчишка», “One Boy, Two Boys”) practically in one breath, and now I am going to recommend it to you.

I do not know how to characterise its genre more precisely: it is a coming-of-age novel, folk horror and topical allegorical absurdity all at once. Thirteen-year-old Andrey is sent off to the army by his wicked stepmother (the morphology of the fairy tale crops up often in this text, do not be surprised), and there everything is somehow strange: the squad includes a couple of dead men, there are no barracks at all, and in their place iron bedsteads stand in a bare field under the open sky.

The further adventures of the “recruits” are more interesting to read than to learn from a retelling, so here I will limit myself to general raptures. Demishkevich’s style is contemporary in the best sense of the word, and this shows not in attempts to mimic the plausible speech of a Russian teenager! I am talking about the complete removal of the boundaries between the reader and what is happening, which are usually, conventionally, represented precisely by the author’s style. The words seem to narrate and comment on the events for the reader all by themselves (and there is plenty in those events to think about and to laugh at). And the ending, in my view, for all its unexpectedness, is more than logical. Short in length, but very palpable in its impressions.
