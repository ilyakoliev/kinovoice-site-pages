Peculiarities of the National Hunt: Digital Detox and Anti-Stress

In short

“Peculiarities of the National Hunt” is a 1995 film directed by Aleksandr Rogozhkin. If I were asked to name films synonymous with the words “digital detox” and “anti-stress”, I would choose these.

BIBetsy Isakova·3 November 2025

About the film

Title
Peculiarities of the National Hunt · Особенности национальной охоты
Director
Aleksandr Rogozhkin
Year
1995

Well, here’s to the classics!

In October I was also studying the universe of Aleksandr Rogozhkin’s films: the three parts of Peculiarities of the National Hunt (in one of the films, of the National Fishing). I had seen fragments of these films on television long ago, but only now did I get round to watching them properly from start to finish. And not in vain: it was one of the best weekends of the month! If I were asked to name films synonymous with the words “digital detox” and “anti-stress”, I would choose these.

These anecdotes about the timeless face of the Russian soul, filmed with the utmost seriousness, surprisingly combine utterly carnivalesque humour with deeply existential philosophy. It is precisely in this paradox that some irrefutable sense of involvement in what happens on screen is born: both in terms of the fun shared with the characters and in terms of the general attitude to the absurdity of life. For this inimitable feeling you want to rewatch and rethink the films, plunging anew into a world without ranks or cares that flares up and fades somewhere on hunting trips in the forest (and also out of self-interest: to memorise for future use all the general’s pithy toasts, each beginning with the fundamental “Well…”)
