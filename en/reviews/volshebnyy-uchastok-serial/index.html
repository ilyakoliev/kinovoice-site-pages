The Magic District, Season 2: The Very Fairy Tale Adults Are Missing

In short

“The Magic District” is a 2023 series directed by Stepan Gordeev. The second season not only takes over all the merits of the first but multiplies them with interest.

BIBetsy Isakova·6 November 2025·updated 28 December 2025

About the series

Title
The Magic District · Волшебный участок
Director
Stepan Gordeev
Year
2023

6 November 2025

One of the best viewing sensations is to start the second season of a series having only just finished the first.

That is what I did with The Magic District, (Volshebny uchastok) having got acquainted with its universe right on the eve of the sequel’s premiere on Okko (the Russian streaming service). It was the best decision: from the first frame you do not want to climb out of the story for a minute, and the season finale gave no chance to relax at all. How pleasant it turned out not to have to wait 2 years for the continuation.

It would have been worth it in any case, though. The second season not only takes over all the merits of the first but multiplies them with interest. Lyokha Popov used to be simply funny in the Perm way; now he is also immortal, with all the gags that follow from it. The Gnome used to be simply a foul-mouthed dwarf (hard to believe, but he is my totem character one hundred percent); now he is not a gnome at all and not even foul-mouthed (and even cooler in his vocabulary, which I will be adding to my lexicon). There used to be the familiar Moscow; now there is a fairy-tale international St Petersburg (with jokes about mushrooms and salt, of which there is never too much (of the jokes)).

There is plenty to admire beyond the plot and the acting. In place are the fairy-tale looks of the characters, stunningly worked out by Anna Bashtova, the rhythmic direction of Stepan Gordeev, the coolest music and the charming atmosphere of the very fairy tale that adults are missing. Falling in love with this project is the best range of emotions you can experience at the end of 2025.

P.S.: As it happens, I also had the rare experience of watching The Magic District and Realnye patsany (“Real Lads”, the long-running Perm sitcom) in parallel, but I will write about that separately some time.

8 December 2025

And in The Magic District everything is as fairy-tale as ever

I returned to watching after a couple of weeks and was very glad to see in episode 5 no fewer than two cool female characters: the Mermaid played by Valentina Mazunina (with the characteristic intonation of Valya from Realnye patsany) and a cameo by Anna Semenovich (in the plot, the singer has been captured by the wicked owner of a wish-granting pike). Once again I was reminded that the charm of the series lies, among other things, in little treats like these in the form of charismatic episodic characters)

I keep waiting to see how the story will approach New Year: after all, the last episode of the season is scheduled for 25 December, and the New Year mood will not create itself! I believe the authors have taken care of it: look, the pike, which is traditionally caught through a hole in the ice, was pulled out of the water in the middle of summer, so maybe some other winter folklore will come our way too.

P.S.: The wisdom tooth that utters those very wisdoms in the voice of Losyash (the moose from the animated series Smeshariki) is the peak. The whole episode I waited for it to say “my fairy-tale friend!”

28 December 2025

However detached The Magic District may be from New Year themes, the series still managed to create a naively festive atmosphere for the season finale. The keys to success are these:

  • Of course, there was no doing without ubiquitous New Year promo. It suits the series, as you can see from the poster.

  • Perhaps Ilya Sobolev’s greeting “Happy New Year”, which was heard at the premiere, meant the new year of 1996 in the story, announced in the finale, rather than the literal holiday, as one might have thought. Well, the final change of era is more of a “Happy Old New Year!” (And yes, the 90s do not annoy me)

  • We love and value New Year films precisely for their naive, life-affirming happy endings. There is one here, and in my opinion it is quite acceptable, if hasty by the standards of the action accelerating towards the resolution. One would like to hope that the wait for the next season will not drag on either)
