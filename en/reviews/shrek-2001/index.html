Shrek in the Original: Every Character Has Their Own Accent

In short

“Shrek” is a 2001 film directed by Andrew Adamson, Vicky Jenson. First of all, of course, Shrek: he has, surprisingly for us, a Scottish accent.

BIBetsy Isakova·15 January 2026

About the film

Title
Shrek
Director
Andrew Adamson, Vicky Jenson
Year
2001

After this revelation, over the holidays Shrek was rewatched in the original, and it turns out that every character there has their own accent! I noticed this from how differently they pronounce the word “donkey”, and after that my linguistic investigation could not be stopped.

First of all, of course, Shrek: he has, surprisingly for us, a Scottish accent. Mike Myers, the original voice actor, wanted in this way to give him a working-class association. Initially the character, like Myers himself, had a Canadian accent, but at the last moment all the lines were re-recorded. Incidentally, in the original Shrek sounds nothing like the Russian dubbing we are used to. He is less peculiar and less matey than in Russian; to my taste, the character even pales a little as a result

Next, Donkey. In the original he has Eddie Murphy’s accent, American colloquial, so-called African-American Vernacular English. In the Russian dubbing, again, this colloquialism does not seem to come across; rather, Donkey just seems like a party-going dude)

Fiona has the golden mean, which is logical. As a representative of two worlds at once, she speaks with a Californian accent. It is associated with progressive young people, sometimes subcultural ones, which in the case of Fiona and her transformations is almost true)

Our favourite son of Snow White and a dwarf, Lord Farquaad, speaks pure British English. Here it really does him no credit: such an accent is chosen only to underline the character’s arrogance and inappropriate pride.

Bonus: in the first Shrek there is a scene of a meeting with Robin Hood in the forest. This noble outlaw speaks with a pronounced French accent, even though he belongs firmly to British folklore. I await your theories, because no sane explanation has been found.
