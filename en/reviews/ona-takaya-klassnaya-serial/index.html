She’s So Cool: Fun, Wild, Absurd, Fresh

In short

“She’s So Cool” is a 2024 series directed by Alexander Domogarov Jr.. A wonderful full stop to end the series: fun, wild, absurd, fresh, what more do you need?

Betsy Isakova·30 July 2024·updated 19 August 2024

About the series

Title
She’s So Cool · Она такая классная
Director
Alexander Domogarov Jr.
Year
2024

30 July 2024

But the main thing that brightened my Monday evening was a live broadcast with a screening of the first episode of She’s So Cool (Ona takaya klassnaya), where Lev Zulkarnaev, Denis Prytkov and the screenwriter Kirill Dyshlovoy were present and commented.

On seeing the post about the broadcast, I immediately dropped episode 7 of House of the Dragon (honestly!), and I do not regret it: the atmosphere was unique, take a look at my screenshot collage. I learned some wonderful details:

  • The slovo patsana (“a lad’s word of honour”) that Denis’s hero gives was invented even before the phrase became iconic thanks to the series of the same name (The Boy’s Word, the 2023 Russian hit series). Life itself brought this line to life!

  • The screenwriter was on set every shooting day, and in film that is a great rarity. Perhaps that is one of the reasons the pilot of the series (as, I hope, the whole of it) came out so vivid

  • The script was born from a chance observation in a café: a boy who was having a lesson with a tutor was quite charmingly distracted by a host of more interesting things around him, loudly announcing it to the whole café

  • Lev’s hero (according to the actor) is very much like Myshkin from Dostoevsky’s novel The Idiot. +1 reason to definitely watch the series

In short, if you are getting ready to watch (I am), be sure to watch the broadcast with the guys, you will get loads of emotions; luckily, it has been saved in full by our colleagues at Kion, a Russian streaming service (many thanks to them for that!)

19 August 2024

The series She’s So Cool is over, but it has left many cool moments in my heart! I have gathered my favourite stills:

*️⃣ Katya (whose old man is a nutcase with a shotgun) and Batya (“the Old Man”, who is actually Egor) in a little house made of bedsheets discussing a future that never came true. My only regret about the series is that Katya never became part of this story; she and Batya would have made a wonderful couple. I hope that on the way back the guys will meet Katya, and this time she will make a different decision. By the way, the actress Maria Gerasimenko, who played Katya, is simply otherworldly: when she appears on screen, she outshines everyone, no question

*️⃣ The trashing of a porn studio in fairy-tale sets and the final demolition of the (fourth) wall. A wonderful full stop to end the series: fun, wild, absurd, fresh, what more do you need?

*️⃣ The funny porn producer, angry at everything and everyone, but trying very hard to stay unflappable. He is, of course, an antihero and a caricature, but he did stir a drop of professional solidarity in me as a producer.

*️⃣ The guys reciting in chorus “What am I to do I have no money at all put that away what are you doing my mum is in the next room oh god yes”. Well, that is simply some kind of poetry performance!

*️⃣ Denis Prytkov’s hero against the backdrop of a Chinese café. The second cool series with him that ends in a Chinese café! (yes, I mean Kapelnik (“The Drip”)). I think I know what I will be expecting from the finale of the next story he is in)
