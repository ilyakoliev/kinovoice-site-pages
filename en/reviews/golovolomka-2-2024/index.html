Inside Out 2: The Device Remains, but No Meanings Have Been Added Around It

In short

“Inside Out 2” is a 2024 film directed by Kelsey Mann. It is great that they not only kept the trick of all these emotions coexisting but added new ones. But it is still predictable and boring.

BIBetsy Isakova·9 July 2024

About the film

Title
Inside Out 2
Director
Kelsey Mann
Year
2024

I watched the second Inside Out. An unpopular opinion is coming (which is not rare for me, though usually in the positive direction), but first some praise.

It is great that they not only kept the trick of all these emotions coexisting but added new ones. Anxiety, of course, is ferociously like the real thing, and Disgust provoked quite palpable disgust. The jokes really are funny and original. And it is pleasing that, together with Riley, the seriousness of the problems the film touches on has grown up: we were shown a panic attack here, if you please.

But it is still predictable and boring. It is extremely hard (for me personally) to empathise with a dazzlingly white, healthy and socialised teenage girl from a complete, well-off and prosperous family living in a developed country. She goes through her growing up without any outside upheavals that would make watching precisely her emotions and moral ideals especially valuable (they are still valuable and important, like any emotions, but they have no cinematic twist). She gets no more and no less traumatic experience than any standard teenager. Actually no, less, because in the finale she is taken into the best girls’ hockey team (and what goes on in the heads of the girls who did not make it??)

And what goes on in the heads of orphans? And what goes on in the heads of teenagers with chronic illnesses? And how do Joy and Anxiety share the console when a teenager’s relatives die? And how is Anger held back in those for whom dyeing a strand of hair red is not enough to be accepted?

I would have liked to ponder possible answers to such questions after watching the expensive and long-awaited sequel to a story with a very original formal device at its heart. But the device remains, and no meanings have been added around it.
