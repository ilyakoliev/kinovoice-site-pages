April X: A Neon Thriller with Connor Storrie

In short

“April X” is a 2025 film directed by Michel K. Parandi. This is a neon psychological thriller with Connor Storrie, who, judging by the trailer, is in for all sorts of scrapes.

BIBetsy Isakova·24 August 2026

About the film

Title
April X
Director
Michel K. Parandi
Year
2025

My personal summer blockbuster season continues, because April X (released in Russia as Kod sna, “Dream Code”) is about to hit cinemas.

This is a neon psychological thriller with Connor Storrie (the man turned a Russian accent into a whole trend, how can you not love him), who, judging by the trailer, is in for all sorts of scrapes. His character Bucks is searching for his missing twin sister April, who can record dreams in digital form. I think we will see fragments of dreams on screen too: the action takes place in the near future, so the science fiction between reality and illusion will definitely be played with.

What is most interesting, the film comes out here 2 weeks earlier than in American cinemas. If only we could get an early look at season 2 of Heated Rivalry like that.

In Russian cinemas from 27 August, courtesy of @leonadistribution
