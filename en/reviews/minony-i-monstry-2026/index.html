Minions & Monsters: Unexpectedly Cinephile Entertainment

In short

“Minions & Monsters” is a 2026 film directed by Pierre Coffin. I did not expect much from the trailer, and what I got was a genuine multi-genre production drama about the Minions making a film.

BIBetsy Isakova·19 August 2026

About the film

Title
Minions & Monsters
Director
Pierre Coffin
Year
2026

Minions & Monsters unexpectedly turned out to be very cinephile entertainment.

The first thing to know: the trailer for the feature about the yellow henchmen of evil gives an impression of a completely different plot from the one you are going to see. I did not expect much from the trailer, and what I got was a genuine multi-genre production drama about the Minions making a film.

I see little point in writing much about the plot: this is the case where you just need to catch the Minions’ vibe, ride this attraction and catch all the cinematic references to Charlie Chaplin, Buster Keaton and anything you associate with 1920s Hollywood. And a bit of backstory of the Minions themselves from the beginning of time is extremely entertaining to learn too: it is a very pleasant buddy-movie part of the whole film)

It remains to work out what the hares from Restlessness are doing in a film about the Minions.
