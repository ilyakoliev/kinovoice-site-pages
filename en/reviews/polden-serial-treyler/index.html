Polden (Noon): Another Strugatsky Adaptation on the Way

In short

This week brought the poster and trailer of the series Polden (“Noon”), another Strugatsky adaptation that we will see this autumn.

BIBetsy Isakova·18 September 2026

This week brought the poster and trailer of the series Polden («Полдень», “Noon”), another adaptation of the Strugatsky brothers that we will see this autumn.

Against that background I remembered that Maxim Matveev had told me in an interview about his character Lev Abalkin:

…I liked Lev Abalkin enormously, because in him there is the tragedy of a man who has been left behind, abandoned, who has turned by chance into someone everyone interprets as a monster <…> He is interesting in his outward features too, to my mind: long hair, black eyes. That is cool! He has turned out a tragically curious character, I think.

The description of the character’s inner world is intriguing enough, so I am waiting for the premiere on 1 October.
