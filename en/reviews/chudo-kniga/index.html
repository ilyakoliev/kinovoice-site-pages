Chudo: A Graphic Novel About an Angel in Yekaterinburg

In short

“Chudo” is a 2026 book by Lyosha Zavoyskaya. This is a small graphic novel (or comic, as you like) about the adventures of an angel who has descended from the heavens to Yekaterinburg.

BIBetsy Isakova·30 August 2026

About the book

Title
Chudo · Чудо
Author
Lyosha Zavoyskaya
Year
2026

If you want to lift your mood, look at the world with renewed love and give your soul a rest, you will need Chudo («Чудо», “Miracle”).

This is a small graphic novel (or comic, as you like) about the adventures of an angel who has descended from the heavens to Yekaterinburg. He longs to see the world of people, and the guides he gets are the spirited Masha and her kind-hearted younger brother Tima. But the most important thing in this comic is the small components that add up to an utterly inimitable whole:

  • A unique style unlike anything else: the golden glow of the angel’s wings, the lights of the night city and the flickering windows of high-rise blocks break through the screen (or the paper). If the notion of “dopamine” can be applied to comics, then it applies to Chudo.

  • A fresh take on the well-established plot of a heavenly being visiting Earth. We have seen it in the film Moy paren — angel (“My Boyfriend Is an Angel”), in the series Good Omens and in many other places. But the adaptation to the ways of a contemporary Russian metropolis, with moments from the life of young people, news on the television, jokes about working in catering and a father who has left the family, feels utterly incomparable.

  • The infectious kindness of what happens, which never slides into a cloying moral about Christian values. I do not know how the author created such an effect, but the plot requires no involvement whatsoever in terms of faith or other sensitive matters: what takes shape before us is simply the story of one acquaintance with Earth and the verdict of a hero who has seen humanity in all its manifestations.

And I have already added Chudo to my list of dream adaptations.
