Kniga Utkina: A Text as Many-Sided as Its Hero

In short

“Kniga Utkina: My tut zhizn zhivyom” is a 2026 book by Stanislav Gridasov (compiler). In short, the text has turned out many-sided, like its hero, and extremely inspiring for great deeds.

BIBetsy Isakova·7 May 2026

About the book

Title
Kniga Utkina: My tut zhizn zhivyom · Книга Уткина: Мы тут жизнь живем
Author
Stanislav Gridasov (compiler)
Year
2026

Hello to everyone whose night went better than mine (I have been unable to fly out of Vnukovo airport since midnight, but that is a whole other story).

What is saving me is the half-autobiography, half-biopic of Vasily Utkin (the legendary Russian sports commentator) called Kniga Utkina: My tut zhizn zhivyom («Книга Уткина: Мы тут жизнь живем», “The Utkin Book: We Live Life Here”). A read completely motley in structure and style, which combines tales from the master’s life, testimony to the birth of sports journalism in the 1990s, and utterly charming details of Utkin’s biography through the eyes of his comrades-in-arms. In short, the text has turned out many-sided, like its hero, and extremely inspiring for great deeds.

One detail clouded my delight, and that is the production of the audio version of Kniga Utkina. I am very sceptical about audiobooks and almost never listen to them, because there is always something I do not like. Here I decided (inspired, apparently, by Vasily’s openness) to listen, and was instantly let down. The beginning of each chapter is dressed up with a peculiar jingle combining jaunty music in the style of the Uralskie Pelmeni opening theme (the Russian TV comedy show) with stock sounds of fans shouting. And this is completely (with all respect to both ingredients) out of keeping with the spirit of the work. The book presents Utkin as a subtle, passionate and witty man who, by misfortune, left us far too early. So hearing such a “playful” compère’s introduction before, say, a philosophical chapter by Yuri Saprykin not only throws off any mood but is simply inappropriate(

So I highly recommend the book, only not to listen to it but precisely to read it.
