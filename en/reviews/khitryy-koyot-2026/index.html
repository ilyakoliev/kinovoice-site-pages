Coyote vs. Acme: A Successful Mix of Animation and Corporate Critique

In short

“Coyote vs. Acme” is a 2026 film directed by Dave Green. Coyote vs. Acme turned out to be the most successful possible mix of unexpected elements: animation and live action, cultural clichés about Albuquerque and a critique of corporations.

BIBetsy Isakova·9 September 2026

About the film

Title
Coyote vs. Acme
Director
Dave Green
Year
2026

Coyote vs. Acme turned out to be the most successful possible mix of unexpected elements: animation and live action, cultural clichés about Albuquerque (thank you, Vince Gilligan) and a critique of corporations.

The plot about a cartoon character who sets out to defend justice (and his tasty dinner) with the help of a human lawyer looks far more interesting on screen than it sounds in a synopsis. The film is the sweetest thing, practically for all ages, with humour that is not primitive yet not bloodthirsty either, and with wonderful casting hits (this is the funniest John Cena I have ever seen). And also a touching story of enmity behind which friendship was actually hiding (I have no occupational deformations whatsoever)

Because the action takes place in Albuquerque, and the lawyer hero is very fond of flashy advertising, I kept wanting to imagine Saul Goodman as played by Bob Odenkirk in his place) But even without that, Coyote vs. Acme is mesmerising: behind its apparent simplicity the film hides great charm. In Russian cinemas from tomorrow, and I strongly recommend going and having a great time.
