Gorbushka. The Heart of Russian Rock: Directing in the Solovyov Manner

In short

About the series

Title
Gorbushka. The Heart of Russian Rock · Горбушка. Сердце русского рока
Director
Timur Fedorov
Year
2026

After watching the documentary concert series Gorbushka. The Heart of Russian Rock, I caught myself thinking that the directing here is of an entirely Solovyov type.

Of course, the connection with the methods of Sergei Solovyov (the director of Assa) lies not in direct echoes but purely in the atmosphere: when, within a single work, the hot somehow starts to combine with the blue and the prickly, that is it. A documentary series about the rockers whose prime years were spent at the Gorbunov House of Culture (the Moscow venue known as Gorbushka) is a video memoir, a concert and even a bit of mysticism with a parallel storyline. And as a bonus, a wonderful opportunity to top up your playlist.

If you love documentaries and genuinely Russian rock, I unequivocally recommend it.
