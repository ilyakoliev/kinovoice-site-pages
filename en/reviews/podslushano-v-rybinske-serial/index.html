Overheard in Rybinsk: Black Humour and Irony Against Nordic Noir

In short

“Overheard in Rybinsk” is a 2025 series directed by Pyotr Todorovsky. That is exactly why the shocking finale (I kept expecting Shirmik to amaze us yet again, and it happened) is doubly impressive.

BIBetsy Isakova·13 March 2025

About the series

Title
Overheard in Rybinsk · Подслушано в Рыбинске
Director
Pyotr Todorovsky
Year
2025

The real charm of Overheard in Rybinsk (Podslushano v Rybinske) is the black humour and irony that keep a twisty, rather gloomy series, even with a touch of mysticism, from turning into a straight-faced Nordic noir, letting it remain a more many-layered work. In which, moreover, the images of the main characters go beyond detective clichés and surprise with unconventional traits.

That is exactly why the shocking finale (I kept expecting Shirmik to amaze us yet again, and it happened) is doubly impressive. It turns out that all this time we were watching the fates of Shirmik and the TV presenter develop in parallel in circumstances that seemingly left no choice. And if the latter rather had no alternatives, though he was not much of a person anyway, Shirmik turned out to be a hostage of his own principles. An excellent transformation by Ruzil Minekaev, it must be said!

Incidentally, the series has an unobtrusive part that gets to the depths of the soul: the soundtrack created by Roman Litvinov (Mujuice), to whose music we have already seen the stories of the characters of Mir! Druzhba! Zhvachka! (“Peace! Friendship! Bubblegum!”) and Kapelnik. (“The Drip”) Only he knows how to combine in sound naivety and a bright hope for the best with the inevitable chthonic murk breaking into our lives from different directions (from our own souls too). It worked 100% and reflected the content of the series exactly: please call on Mujuice to score series more often.
