Spider-Man: Brand New Day: Feeling on a Par with a Superhero

In short

“Spider-Man: Brand New Day” is a 2026 film directed by Destin Daniel Cretton. The adventures of Spider-Man in a world where he loses not only his external but also his internal supports look more than plausible.

BIBetsy Isakova·3 August 2026

About the film

Title
Spider-Man: Brand New Day
Director
Destin Daniel Cretton
Year
2026

Spider-Man: Brand New Day lets every viewer feel on a par with a superhero.

Doing so could not be easier, because in the new instalment Peter Parker appears as someone starting over from scratch, slightly burnt out and, like any young adult, confused. It seems the friendly neighbour could not have been any different in 2026: this new sincerity in Tom Holland’s portrayal is winning at the very least, and at most it reboots the franchise perfectly. Let me remind you that after the previous instalment, as a result of Doctor Strange’s spell, everyone in the world (even those closest to the hero) forgot Peter Parker.

The adventures of Spider-Man in a world where he loses not only his external but also his internal supports look more than plausible. The most varied special effects are, of course, in place and at their best, but Parker’s flights between skyscrapers look convincing not only thanks to the cool graphics. Holland has made his Spider-Man not just a “neighbour” but a true contemporary, who learns to find support within himself and to give it to others, even when it seems the critical point has come. But he does his superheroing: turning enemies into friends, emptiness into connections, and all of it to a cool soundtrack.

Maybe, in 2026, that is the main superpower.
