Mayak 2025 Festival: Jury, Programme, Results and Impressions

In short

Mayak 2025 was the third edition, but the first for me, and I am very happy to have become part of this festival — it is a big, long celebration of cinema that brings its own surprises to every viewer.

Betsy Isakova·11 September 2025·updated 12 October 2025

11 September 2025

The morning begins with festival news — Mayak (the Russian film festival in Gelendzhik) has announced its jury!

The feature competition will be judged by:

• Bakur Bakuradze, jury chair, director, producer, screenwriter (and director of the festival’s opening film Lermontov) • Eduard Gimpel, cinematographer • Evgenia Gromova, actress • Natalya Kudryashova, director, actress, screenwriter • Sergey Melkumov, producer

And the short films are in the hands of:

• Sergey Kornikhin, producer • Anna Kuznetsova, director, screenwriter (and director of Intermission, which won Mayak 2023) • Ekaterina Smolina, cinematographer

All that is left is a small matter: finding out the programme.

23 September 2025

The Mayak festival now has its programme.

I have special expectations for the film Yura Was Here with Khabensky and Kotrelev and for the animated film Tied Up from Konstantin Bronzit.

And I am also waiting, ironic as it may sound, for The Good Life — by the look of it, it unexpectedly plays on Gaidar’s story Golubaya chashka («Голубая чашка», “The Blue Cup”), and the lead is the theatre star Serafima Goshchanskaya.

9 October 2025

Mayak 2025 results:

  • Grand Prix — Yura Was Here
  • Best Director — Sonya Rayzman, Scenes of Friendly Ties
  • Best Debut — Fireworks in the Afternoon
  • Best Actor — Konstantin Khabensky (Yura Was Here)
  • Best Actress — Maria Karpova (Scenes of Friendly Ties)
  • Best Screenplay — Ekaterina Tirdatova (Above Eternal Rest)
  • Best Cinematography — Oleg Lukichev (Above Eternal Rest)
  • Best Short Film — Igra v pryatki («Игра в прятки», “Hide and Seek”)

9 October 2025

I guessed 4 categories right! Not bad, and the element of surprise survived.

10 October 2025

The last, but not the least.

Mayak 2025 was the third edition, but the first for me, and I am very happy to have become part of this festival — it is a big, long celebration of cinema that brings its own surprises to every viewer.

The video shows the Mayak team, who spend the whole year preparing for the festival: that many people fit neither on the stage nor in the lens (but I tried). I am grateful to every one of these professionals for the fact that the festival takes place, that films reach us and that the dialogue between viewers and filmmakers happens.

The festival’s programme director Stas Tyrkin has a truly amazing ability to put together extraordinary combinations of cinematic works. This year we kept catching parallels between the films: in the relationships between mothers and sons, in attitudes to cats, in close-ups of ears of wheat symbolising the hero’s inner world and, of course, in the title of the closing series Lighthouse (in Russian Mayak, like the festival), which will become a bridge between this year and the next. Bravo!

12 October 2025

The final chord of Mayak 2025 is a digest by Nikita and me with, as it happened, all the films about parent–child relationships (including the closing series Lighthouse).

Great things are seen from a distance — in this selection the subtle connections between the films of the programme are more noticeable than ever. I recommend reading it.
