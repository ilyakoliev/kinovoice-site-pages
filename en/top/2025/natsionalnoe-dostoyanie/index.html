Plot

Only one of them will become the master of the art.

Nagasaki, 1964. Following the death of his Yakuza father, the leader of a yakuza gang, gifted Kikuo is taken under the wing of famed Kabuki actor. Alongside the great performer's only son, Shunsuke, he dedicates his life to the traditional artform. Over the ensuing decades, the destiny of the two young disciples entwines and twists – and only one will become the greatest Japanese master of Kabuki.
