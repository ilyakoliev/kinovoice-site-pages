Plot

Set against the picturesque landscapes of Turkey, Arafta unravels a tale of love forged in the flames of vengeance. Ateş, a man haunted by childhood scars and consumed by rage, returns to destroy the family that shattered his life years ago. His weapon is not a sword, but a marriage: 187 days with Mercan, the daughter of his enemy.
