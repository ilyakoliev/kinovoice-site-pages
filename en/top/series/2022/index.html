How the list is made

The list has the TV series that premiered this year and were rated by at least a thousand people on both Kinopoisk and IMDb. A series has one rating for all its seasons. Talk shows and reality TV are left out.

The score on a card averages the two ratings, weighted by the number of votes: the site where more people voted counts more, but never more than three quarters. The rank also accounts for how many people voted, so a series with a couple of thousand enthusiastic votes does not overtake one with hundreds of thousands. The data is refreshed roughly once a month.
