Plot

Honeymoon is a 2022 Kannada romantic-comedy series following a timid man and an outspoken woman navigating their arranged marriage during a 6-night trip to Kerala. The six-episode Voot Select series, featuring Nagabhushana and Sanjana Anand, is praised for its relatable, non-preachy approach to relationship dynamics and natural performances.
