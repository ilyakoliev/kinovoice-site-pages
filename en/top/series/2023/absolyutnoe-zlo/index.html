Plot

16-year-old Erik, used to violence both at home and in the schoolyard, is expelled from his Stockholm school due to his violent behaviour. Consequently, he is transferred to the boarding school Stjernsberg, his last chance to continue his education and break with his previous life, but at Stjernsberg, the violence is worse than at home.
