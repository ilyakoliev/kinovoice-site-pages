Reviews of festival and theatrical premieres, coverage of series and streaming, theatre, books and festival notes. Kinovoice grew out of the Telegram channel kino_voice and publishes in Russian; selected texts appear in English.

Editor-in-chief

Betsy Isakova is a film critic and media manager. She writes for Kinopoisk (Russia’s largest film database and media), Okko, The Blueprint, RBC Style and Afisha Daily: year-end lists of the best films, series and books, coverage of the Pilot series festival, reviews and interviews with actors and series creators. For almost three years she produced Kinopoisk’s film podcast Krupnym Planom (“Close-Up”). She has run the Telegram channel kino_voice since May 2023.
